Lenses and Optical Power

Optics · #142

Short version

A lens redirects light by refraction. A converging lens brings paraxial parallel rays toward a real focus, while a diverging lens spreads them as if they came from a virtual focus. Optical power describes how strongly a thin lens bends light and is measured in dioptres.
